- **Choose an experiment.** Inspect recorded observations and transitions, form hypotheses about the game's rules, and choose actions that test an uncertain mechanism or make progress toward solving the game.
- **Write a controller.** Edit `controller.py`. Describe the experiment's goal in its module docstring and define `get_controller()` returning an object with `act(obs)` and `is_done(obs)`. Both receive the full observation dictionary. `act` returns one action in the game's format; `is_done` returns True to stop before the next action. Keeping `is_done` as False is valid: the environment or framework limits can stop the call.
- **Run and refine.** Call `RunController` to execute your controller in the real environment. Inspect its feedback and the newly recorded experience, revise your understanding and controller, then choose the next experiment.
